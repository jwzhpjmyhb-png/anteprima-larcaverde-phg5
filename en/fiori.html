Flowers

Flowering plants, all year round

Alongside the Christmas collection, we supply florists, nurseries and shops with flowering pot plants for every season: hydrangeas, cyclamens, azaleas and roses.

HydrangeasCyclamensAzaleasRoses
Spring and summer

Hydrangeas

Great spheres of petals, light as clouds: a single hydrangea is enough to fill a shop window, a terrace or a garden. In some varieties the colour reveals the soil: it turns blue in acidic ground and pink in chalky ground.

Flowering
From late spring to summer
Position
Partial shade, soil kept moist
Colours
Blue, pink, white

Autumn and winter

Cyclamens

When it is cold outside, the cyclamen lights up the shop: upswept petals like small flames and heart-shaped leaves traced with silver. It flowers for a long time, bringing colour in exactly the months when other flowers rest.

Flowering
From autumn to late winter
Position
Bright light out of direct sun, cool room
Colours
Red, pink, fuchsia, white
Ask about availability
Late winter and spring

Azaleas

A compact bush that vanishes beneath its flowers, single or double, in vivid coral and red or soft pink and white. It is one of the best-loved gifts from late winter into spring, at home and in the shop window.

Flowering
From late winter to spring
Position
Plenty of light, acidic compost, lime-free water
Colours
Pink, coral, red, white
Ask about availability
From spring to autumn

Roses

Full, cup-shaped blooms, petals soft as silk and a scent you notice as soon as you lean in. Repeat-flowering varieties renew themselves, bud after bud, throughout the warm season.

Flowering
From late spring to autumn
Position
Full sun, at least six hours a day
Colours
Apricot, pink, cream
