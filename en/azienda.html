About us

A family story that grows every Christmas

L'ArcaVerde was born in Terni from the Arca family's passion for plants, and grew one van at a time into a wholesale supplier for florists and nurseries.

Our story
  1. Early 1980s

    Alessandro Arca starts the business with a single van, supplying shops and nurseries.

  2. Mid-1980s

    The idea is born: the decorated Christmas tree, a living conifer dressed for the holidays.

  3. Today

    Alessandro, Stefano and Andrea Arca run the company; our own vehicles deliver across Central and Northern Italy.
