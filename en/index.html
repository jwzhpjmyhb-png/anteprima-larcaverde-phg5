Who we are

From our nursery to shop windows

It all started in the early 1980s with a single van and a lot of passion. In the mid-1980s came the idea that made our name: the decorated Christmas tree, a living conifer dressed for the holidays.

Today the Arca brothers run the company together, and our own vehicles supply shops and nurseries across Central and Northern Italy.
