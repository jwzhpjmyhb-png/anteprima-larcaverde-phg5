Privacy notice

Privacy & cookies

In short: no profiling cookies, no analytics, no data sent by the forms unless you decide to.

Who handles your data

The data controller is L'ArcaVerde S.r.l., Voc. La Selva, 05100 Terni (TR), Italy, VAT no. 01509760557. For any request: info@larcaverde.com · +39 0744 303773.

Forms and request list

The website forms do not send data to a server: they open your email app with the message already written, and you decide whether to send it. We use the data you email us (name, company, contact details, requested models) only to reply and prepare a quote, and keep it for as long as needed to handle the request and any orders, and for as long as the law requires.

Cookies and browser storage

The website uses no profiling cookies and no analytics. It only saves your request list and chosen language in your browser, so you don't lose them between pages: they stay on your device, are never sent to us and can be deleted at any time from your browser settings. The fonts are hosted on the website itself.

Google map

The map on the Contact page only loads if you press “Show the map”. Google may then set its own cookies according to its privacy policy.

Technical data

As with any website, the hosting service records technical browsing data (such as the IP address) only for security and operation.

Your rights

You may at any time ask to access, correct or erase your data, restrict its use or object (Articles 15–22 of EU Regulation 2016/679) by writing to info@larcaverde.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority.
